## Veterans, rookies, and the attrition question

The description presents a deliberate contrast: build squads of veterans, or rush opponents with rookies. This is a real strategic tension in most games that offer it, and the underlying logic does not require published numbers.

Veterans represent accumulated investment. If a levelled pilot is lost, the cost is not just the unit but the time spent improving them. Rookies are cheap in that sense — losing one presumably stings less — but they may perform worse in a fight. The practical question is where you can afford to spend lives. A defensive holding action may be a reasonable place to absorb losses with inexpensive units, while a decisive assault may be where you want your experienced pilots.

The store page does not state experience gain rates, whether pilots can die permanently, or whether rookies can be promoted into veterans. Third-party guides may speculate about this, but they are secondary observations and should be treated as such. Verify in game.
